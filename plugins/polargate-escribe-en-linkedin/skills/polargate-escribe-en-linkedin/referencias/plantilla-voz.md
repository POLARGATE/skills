# Voice & Brand Profile

Your voice, niche, and rules in one place. This is the empty template: your
filled copy lives in your project as `linkedin/voz.md`. Fill it once (or let
`quitar-pinta-de-ia` build it from a few of your real posts), and every guide in
this skill reads it before drafting, so you stop re-explaining who you are on
every request. Edit it freely, keep it as long or short as you like. The skill
never publishes it: Claude reads it in your session to steer the drafts, like
anything else you share with Claude.

> With your OK the skill creates `linkedin/voz.md` from this template and adds
> it to `.gitignore` first, so a push does not carry it.

Skills only load this profile when `filled: yes` below. An empty template is
ignored, so drafts fall back to the generic voice rules until you populate it.
