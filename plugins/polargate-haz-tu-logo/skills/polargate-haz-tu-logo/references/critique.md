# Critique: be the design director, kill weak work before the user sees it

The difference between a generator and a studio is the critique loop. A junior shows everything; a director shows only what survives a hard rubric. Run this in phase 3 and again before any presentation. Be honest and a little merciless; the user benefits.

## The 7-axis rubric

Score each concept 1 to 5 on each axis. Be specific about why, not just a number.

1. Distinctive: unlike the category and unownable by competitors. A 5 could not have its name swapped for a rival's. A 1 is a stock symbol. This is the most important axis.
2. Simple: one idea, fewest elements that express it. A 5 is irreducible. A 1 has competing ideas or fussy detail.
3. Scalable: survives the favicon (16px) and squint tests. A 5 is unmistakable at 16px in one color. A 1 turns to mush.
4. Relevant: true to the brand attributes and context, without being literal. A 5 feels inevitable for this brand. A 1 is generic or off-tone.
5. Memorable: a clear, recallable silhouette someone could sketch from memory. A 5 has a hook. A 1 is forgettable.
6. Timeless: not chained to a passing trend. A 5 will look intentional in ten years. A 1 is this-year's-fad shaped.
7. Versatile: works mono, reversed, on light and dark, small and large, still and in motion, across the variant matrix. A 5 is robust everywhere. A 1 only works in one treatment.

## The gate

- Any concept scoring 1 or 2 on Distinctive or Scalable is rejected outright, regardless of how pretty it is. These two are non-negotiable.
- Aim to advance only concepts averaging 4 or higher, with no axis below 3.
- If a whole batch is weak, do not lower the bar. Return to ideation (`references/strategy-and-ideation.md`) and find a sharper idea. A weak batch usually means a weak idea, not weak execution.

## Director's red flags (instant suspicion)

- You needed a sentence to explain the hidden meaning. Cut it.
- It resembles a mark you have seen before. Distinct enough to avoid a cease-and-desist?
- The idea is rung-1 literal (see metaphor laddering). Push to abstract.
- It only works in the color version. Rebuild mono-first.
- The detail is decoration, not meaning. Remove it.
- It is faceless minimal with no hook, or cluttered with two ideas. Find the single meaningful detail.
- It could be any company in the category. Move away from a category default.

## What to show the user

Present only survivors, each with its one-line idea and its rubric scores (this builds trust and teaches taste). Order them so the strongest and most distinctive lead, with safer, more conventional options after, so the user sees the range and the recommendation. Say which one you would ship and why. Never bury a strong concept under five weak ones to pad the count; 3 sharp concepts beat 9 mediocre ones.
